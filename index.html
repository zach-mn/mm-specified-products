About Mike Moore

President, M&M Specified Products

Leave things better than you found them.

Mike's guiding principle
Member, Water & Sewer Distributors of America. Served on the Educational Scholarship Committee and the Associates Board.

Mike Moore is the President of M&M Specified Products, headquartered in Woodbury, Minnesota. With more than 25 years of experience in the underground utility industry, Mike has dedicated his career to advancing the infrastructure that communities rely on every day. His expertise spans water and sewer systems, telecommunications, stormwater management, and natural gas distribution, making him a trusted resource and respected leader throughout the industry.

Throughout his career, Mike has been driven by a passion for innovation, safety, and improving industry standards. He is the holder of multiple patents related to complete tracer wire systems for underground utilities and played a key role in developing the Complete Tracer Wire Specification currently housed by the Minnesota Rural Water Association. His contributions have helped establish best practices that improve the reliability, locate ability, and long-term performance of underground utility systems.

Before building his successful career in the utility industry, Mike proudly served his country as a member of the United States Army during the Gulf War. His commitment to service continued at home, where he spent seven years as a volunteer firefighter and EMT in Woodbury, Minnesota, helping protect and serve his community during times of need.

Mike has also been actively involved in industry leadership and professional development. As a member of the Water & Sewer Distributors of America (WASDA), he served on both the Educational Scholarship Committee and the Associates Board, helping support the next generation of industry professionals while strengthening partnerships across the water and wastewater sectors.

At the heart of Mike's success is his family. He has been married to his wife, Laurie, for more than 40 years. Together they have raised two sons, both now married, and are proud grandparents to a grandson, with a granddaughter soon to join the family. Mike credits much of his personal and professional success to the support, values, and encouragement of his family.

Whether serving his country, supporting his community, mentoring industry professionals, or developing innovative solutions for underground infrastructure, Mike's guiding principle has remained the same: leave things better than you found them. His career stands as a testament to leadership, service, innovation, and a commitment to helping others succeed.
