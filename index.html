About Us

M&M Specified Products LLC was founded on the principle of supplying the highest quality service and products to our clients in the water, sewer, irrigation, and municipal marketplace.

Our team brings decades of combined experience in the industry, providing knowledgeable representation for leading manufacturers. We work closely with engineers, contractors, and municipalities to deliver the right solutions for every project.

Headquartered in the Midwest, we proudly serve customers across the region with responsive, hands-on support from specification through installation.
